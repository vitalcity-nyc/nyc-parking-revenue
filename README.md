# NYC street parking: the map and the math

A Vital City interactive on the economics of New York City's curb. Maps the city's ~85,000 metered spaces, models what pricing the rest could raise, and compares peer cities.

The ~1 million parkable-space estimate at its core comes from original analysis by the Sam Schwartz Transportation Research Program, prepared for Vital City. Revenue models build on published work by Donald Shoup, Rachel Weinberger (Regional Plan Association), Transportation Alternatives, the Center for an Urban Future and Hunter College's Urban Policy & Planning program.

- Live: https://vitalcity-nyc.github.io/nyc-parking-revenue/
- Embed (for a Vital City story page): append `?embed=1` to the URL

Data: NYC Open Data — Centerline (inkn-q76z), Parking Meters (693u-uax6). Parkable-space estimate: Sam Schwartz Transportation Research Program memo and workbook to Vital City, June 2026.
